Community healthcare support · Abeokuta

Someone needs help. Someone else can help.

CrowdAid helps people facing healthcare costs reach beyond their own circle, to neighbours across Abeokuta who can give money, practical help or their time.

Get help For yourself or someone you care for Donate, offer practical help or volunteer
Donate to a campaignGive money towards someone's treatment› Offer practical assistanceTransport, meals, blood donation, equipment or skills› Volunteer with CrowdAidJoin outreaches and community fundraising›

How CrowdAid works

  1. Step 1Tell us what you needRequest support for yourself or someone you care for. It's free and takes a few minutes. No documents are needed to start.
  2. Step 2Reach beyond your circleWith your consent, CrowdAid shares your story with people across Abeokuta, not only the friends and family you already know.
  3. Step 3Get community supportPeople donate online, offer practical help like transport or meals, and raise funds in person at CrowdAid outreaches.
✓Every request is reviewed by the CrowdAid team before anything is published. Support comes from the community, so we can't promise how much any campaign will raise.
